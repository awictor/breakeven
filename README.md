# BreakEven 🟠

**Business break-even calculator.** Enter your fixed costs, price, and variable cost to see exactly how many units (and how much revenue) you must sell to cover costs — plus your contribution margin and margin of safety. Runs 100% in the browser, offline.

![version](https://img.shields.io/badge/version-0.1.0-d97706) ![status](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-live-d97706) [![CI](https://github.com/awictor/breakeven/actions/workflows/ci.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/awictor/breakeven/actions/workflows/ci.yml)

## Features

- **Break-even units & revenue** from fixed costs, price, and variable cost
- **Contribution margin** per unit and as a %
- **Profit at your volume** and **margin of safety** (how far sales can fall before a loss)
- Shareable link, dark mode, local persistence, zero dependencies — one `index.html`

Part of [Toolkit](https://awictor.github.io/toolkit/).
